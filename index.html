Do you offer massage school or a massage core program?

The free Leah practice roadmap is for massage-school students and practitioners. The packages on this site focus on business infrastructure—branding, websites, booking, intake, reminders, and follow-up—not massage-school coursework, a clinical core curriculum, or hands-on training. For a school's specific program, check its official curriculum.

Can a neuromuscular massage therapist use Leah?

Yes. The practice website, booking flow, intake forms, reminders, and client follow-up can be organized around a therapist's own services and policies. Leah and SomaSync AI support the business side; they do not provide treatment, diagnosis, or clinical advice.

Do you work with massage therapists in Northern California?

SomaSync AI works online with practice owners in Northern California and beyond, including throughout California and the wider United States. It provides digital practice systems—not in-person massage services or a local clinic.
