# Leah + SomaSync AI — broader marketing approach

## Core position
**Your work deserves a real online home.** Leah + SomaSync AI help independent service providers turn a skill, service or offer into a recognizable digital storefront and a client journey they can learn and own.

**Primary promise:** Get discovered, build trust, make booking easier, and follow up consistently—without having to assemble the whole tech stack alone.

**Audience:** artists and creators; influencers and models; independent small-business owners; barbers and estheticians; massage/bodywork practitioners; physical therapists and other service professionals. Keep the offer itself at the center; tailor examples by trade rather than pretending one template fits everyone.

## Message sequence
1. **Brand it:** clarify the offer, name, visual identity, and service pages.
2. **Make it bookable:** connect the business presence to schedule, policies, inquiries, and appropriate intake.
3. **Keep the relationship:** confirmations, reminders, post-visit check-ins, and rebooking touchpoints.
4. **Hand over control:** explain the client's schedule, list, access, and operating workflow; help them get comfortable owning it.

## Suggested headline and call to action
- Headline: **Your work deserves a real online home. Build your brand. Book your people.**
- Supporting line: **We take the guesswork out of getting online—then help you learn the system and keep your calendar and client relationships yours.**
- Primary CTA: **Sketch My Brand** or **Talk Through My Business**.

## Trust-sensitive identity checks
Some businesses may need client identity verification before an appointment. Describe ID photo + live face capture as a **discovery/scoping conversation**, not a currently deployed feature, until a provider and full workflow are confirmed. Before offering it, define the vendor, what is captured, consent and accessibility alternatives, matching decision/review, retention/deletion, security, and applicable legal/privacy obligations. Do not conflate it with SomaSyncAI's therapist pre-session assessment.

## Keep the two product journeys distinct
- **Leah + SomaSync AI (`leah.somasyncai.com`):** branding and business infrastructure for independent service providers.
- **SomaSyncAI (`somasyncai.com`):** therapist clinical documentation product; the public intake preview is a pre-session assessment. Its beta notice says to use only synthetic/de-identified information and is not an identity-verification feature.

## Rollout ideas
- Publish trade-specific examples (barber, artist, esthetician, therapist) using real consented client work, not fabricated case-study outcomes.
- Use short social content: “From social bio to bookable storefront,” “what happens after someone books,” and “your client list is an asset you own.”
- Capture leads with a low-pressure brand sketch or business workflow consultation; segment by service type before quoting scope.
- Track qualified inquiries, completed consults, booking conversion, client return rate where customers consent to measurement, and referral source. Avoid guaranteed growth/retention claims.

## Current limitations to state clearly
The existing package pages and proof case are therapist-oriented. General-purpose packages/pricing, cross-industry templates, and the ID/live-face check flow should be confirmed before implying they are already available. The October promotion language was removed from the homepage to avoid treating a dated offer as evergreen.
